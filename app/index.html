⚠ GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies. The only confirmed missable trophy is Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at Lucia's balcony spot in Chapter 1 before the chapter ends.

Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

Five phases, in order. Finish the story first, then strangers, then collectibles, then activities, then the crime-spree cleanup — and 100% completion (A Long Road) falls just before the platinum. About 200 hours, one playthrough, all offline.
